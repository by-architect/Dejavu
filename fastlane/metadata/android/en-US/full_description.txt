Dejavu is a web browser for Android built on Mozilla's Gecko engine, the same
engine Firefox uses. It keeps Firefox's rendering, add-ons and account sync, and
replaces the way tabs are organised with something quieter.

Instead of one long list of tabs, Dejavu gives you:

* Workspaces - separate sets of tabs for separate parts of your life, each with
  its own colour theme and icon.
* Containers - keep sites signed in to different accounts apart, per workspace.
* Pinned tabs and essentials - the pages you always want open, always in reach.
* Folders - group tabs inside a workspace and collapse them out of the way.
* Split view - two pages side by side.
* A single bottom bar with every action, and an address field that stays out of
  the way at the top.

Sync: Dejavu signs in with your Mozilla account and syncs workspaces, pinned
tabs, essentials, folders and containers with Zen Browser on the desktop, next
to the usual bookmarks, history and passwords.

Private by default: telemetry, studies, the daily usage ping and search
suggestions are all off from the first launch. Nothing is sent anywhere to make
the browser work.
