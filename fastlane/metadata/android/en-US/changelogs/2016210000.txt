Dejavu now syncs with Firefox too: each of your computers and phones with Firefox gets its own workspace with its pinned tabs and tab groups, and a tab you open or close there opens or closes in Firefox. Choose Firefox or Zen on the account page. After an update, the tour shows what changed, and What's new stays at the top of Settings, next to links to the author and to Buy me a coffee. Dejavu also uses less memory and the app is smaller.
