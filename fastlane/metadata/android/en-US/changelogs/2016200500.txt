Tabs you have not used for a while now sleep to free memory. Every tab action is in one list where you choose where it shows, and the actions bar below pages is yours to edit. Folders stay open or closed the way you left them and show how many tabs are open inside. History can be grouped by workspace and folder. Dejavu now warns you about dangerous sites, has its own look for dialogs and menus, and syncs pinned tabs with Zen Browser more reliably.
